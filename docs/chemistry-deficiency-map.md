## 1. Skill Area Taxonomy

The secondary chemistry curriculum is structured into six fundamental cognitive and practical domains:

1. **STOICHIOMETRY_EQUATIONS**: Chemical formulas, valency, balancing equations, ionic equations, mole calculations, percentage composition, empirical and molecular formulas, limiting reagents, and gas volumes.
2. **ATOMIC_PERIODICITY**: Subatomic particles, electronic configurations ($s, p, d, f$), periodic trends (atomic and ionic radii, ionization energy, electron affinity, electronegativity), and chemical bonding (electrovalent, covalent, coordinate, metallic, intermolecular).
3. **PHYSICAL_CHEMISTRY**: Kinetic theory of matter, gas laws, chemical energetics, enthalpy profile diagrams, rates of reaction and collision theory, chemical equilibrium, and Le Chatelier's principle.
4. **INORGANIC_CHEMISTRY**: Acids, bases, salts, pH scale, solubility, precipitation, properties of metals and their extraction (metallurgy of iron and aluminium), non-metals and their industrial compounds (carbon, nitrogen, sulphur, chlorine, and ammonia).
5. **ORGANIC_CHEMISTRY**: IUPAC nomenclature, homologous series, functional groups, structural isomerism, reactions of hydrocarbons (alkanes, alkenes, alkynes), alkanols, alkanoic acids, esters, fats, oils, and synthetic/natural polymers.
6. **ANALYTICAL_PRACTICAL**: Volumetric analysis (titration technique, meniscus reading, indicator selection, titration calculations, concordancy $\pm 0.20\text{ cm}^3$), qualitative analysis (cation and anion identification, confirmatory gas tests), and laboratory safety precautions.

## 3. Teacher Review Gate & Examination Alignment Notice

Every course, module, lesson, question, and diagnostic item in the Trainbow HUB Chemistry Academy carries a `reviewStatus` of `"needs_teacher_review"`. 

- **Calculations & Data Transparency:** All numerical answers are computed using the unit-aware Chemistry computational tools (`tools-service` and ChemPy/SymPy) and verified by independent stoichiometric second-calculation checks.
- **Physical Constants & Convention:** In accordance with WAEC and JAMB exam instructions:
  - Molar volume of a gas at s.t.p. = $22.4\text{ dm}^3/\text{mol}$ ($22,400\text{ cm}^3/\text{mol}$)
  - Avogadro constant $L = 6.02 \times 10^{23}\text{ mol}^{-1}$
  - Faraday constant $F = 96,500\text{ C/mol}$
  - Relative atomic masses are explicitly stated in each lesson constants table (e.g. $\text{H}=1, \text{C}=12, \text{N}=14, \text{O}=16, \text{Na}=23, \text{Mg}=24, \text{Al}=27, \text{S}=32, \text{Cl}=35.5, \text{K}=39, \text{Ca}=40, \text{Fe}=56, \text{Cu}=64, \text{Zn}=65, \text{Ag}=108$).
- **Safety First:** Practical lessons carry a prominent warning that chemical experiments must only be performed under supervision in a certified secondary laboratory. Videos depicting hazardous, explosive, or non-educational demonstrations are strictly prohibited by allowlist and blocklist rules.
