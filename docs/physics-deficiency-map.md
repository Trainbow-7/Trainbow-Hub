# Trainbow HUB: Physics Weak Area Deficiency Map & Examiner Evidence

## 1. Overview & Pedagogical Framework
This deficiency map provides an evidenced, curriculum-aligned analysis of where West African (WAEC WASSCE, NECO SSCE) and Nigerian national (JAMB UTME) secondary school candidates systematically drop marks in Physics. 

Every course, module, lesson, and diagnostic question within the **Trainbow HUB Physics Academy** is explicitly cross-referenced to one or more of these evidenced deficiencies.

All items adhere strictly to the **Teacher Review Gate**:
- `reviewStatus = "needs_teacher_review"`
- `confidence = "high"` (grounded in published Chief Examiner reports and official syllabus specifications) or `"needs teacher input"` (where school-level empirical variance requires validation).

---

## 2. Regulatory & Curriculum Evidence Sources
1. **WAEC Chief Examiners' Reports (Physics Paper 2 Theory & Paper 3 Practical)** (2018–2024):
   - Detailed candidate weaknesses, frequent arithmetic and dimensional errors, ray diagram omissions, and poor graphical analysis.
2. **JAMB UTME Physics Syllabus & IBASS System** (2024–2026):
   - Speed problem-solving specifications, dimensional analysis, CBT pacing, and multi-step deduction.
3. **NECO SSCE Physics Examiners' Annual Overviews** (2019–2024):
   - Step-by-step method marks, conversion slips, and electrical circuit deductions.
4. **NERDC Senior Secondary Physics Curriculum** (SS 1–3):
   - Core behavioral objectives, experimental investigations, and national examination benchmark standards.

### Skill Area 1: Mechanics, Motion & Forces (MCH)

| Code | Title | Levels | Evidence Source | Confidence | Priority |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **WA-PHY-MCH-01** | Units, Conversions, Dimensions & Dimensional Homogeneity | SS 1-3, WAEC, JAMB | WAEC Chief Examiner Report (Paper 2) 2021-2024; JAMB IBASS | High | **Yes** |
| *Description* | Candidates fail to convert non-SI units ($g \to kg$, $cm \to m$, $mins \to s$, $km/h \to m/s$) before substituting into kinematic or dynamical formulas. Inability to derive dimensional expressions ($[M][L][T]^{-2}$) to verify physical homogeneity. |
| **WA-PHY-MCH-02** | Vectors vs Scalars & Component Resolution | SS 1-3, WAEC, JAMB | WAEC Chief Examiner Report (Physics 2) 2020-2023 | High | **Yes** |
| *Description* | Treating vector quantities (displacement, velocity, force, momentum) as simple algebraic scalars. Failure to resolve inclined forces into perpendicular components ($F_x = F \cos \theta$, $F_y = F \sin \theta$) when solving inclined planes or equilibrium systems. |
| **WA-PHY-MCH-03** | Motion Graphs: Velocity-Time & Displacement-Time Interpretation | SS 1-2, WAEC, JAMB | WAEC Chief Examiner Report (Paper 2) 2019-2024 | High | **Yes** |
| *Description* | Inability to deduce acceleration from graph gradients ($\Delta v / \Delta t$) or total distance travelled from area under the velocity-time curve. Confusing curved displacement-time graphs (variable velocity) with linear uniform motion. |
| **WA-PHY-MCH-04** | Newton's Laws & Free-Body Force Equilibrium | SS 1-3, WAEC, JAMB | WAEC Chief Examiner Report 2022; NECO SSCE 2023 | High | **Yes** |
| *Description* | Omitting weight ($W = mg$), normal reaction ($R = mg \cos \theta$), or friction ($F_r = \mu R$) in free-body diagrams on horizontal and inclined planes. Stating Newton's 3rd Law without noting that action and reaction act on *different* bodies. |
| **WA-PHY-MCH-05** | Projectile Motion: Independence of Horizontal & Vertical Components | SS 2-3, WAEC, JAMB | JAMB UTME Physics Syllabus; WAEC Paper 2 | High | **Yes** |
| *Description* | Mixing vertical gravitational acceleration ($g$) with horizontal motion where velocity remains constant ($u_x = u \cos \theta$). Forgetting that at maximum height, vertical velocity component $v_y = 0$, leading to incorrect time of flight calculations. |
| **WA-PHY-MCH-06** | Uniform Circular Motion & Centripetal Acceleration | SS 2-3, WAEC, JAMB | WAEC Chief Examiner Report 2021; JAMB IBASS | High | No |
| *Description* | Believing centripetal force ($F = \frac{mv^2}{r}$) is an additional external force rather than the resultant force directed toward the centre. Miscalculating angular velocity ($\omega = 2\pi f = \frac{v}{r}$). |
| **WA-PHY-MCH-07** | Work, Energy, Power & Mechanical Energy Conservation | SS 1-3, WAEC, JAMB | WAEC Chief Examiner Report 2023; NECO SSCE 2022 | High | **Yes** |
| *Description* | Calculating work done without multiplying by the cosine of the angle between force and displacement ($W = F d \cos \theta$). Overlooking energy dissipation due to friction when applying the principle of conservation of mechanical energy ($mgh = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 + W_f$). |
| **WA-PHY-MCH-08** | Linear Momentum, Collisions & Sign Conventions | SS 1-3, WAEC, JAMB | WAEC Chief Examiner Report 2020-2024; JAMB IBASS | High | **Yes** |
| *Description* | Failure to assign opposite algebraic signs to velocities moving in opposite directions prior to or after collision ($m_1 u_1 - m_2 u_2$). Confusing elastic collisions (kinetic energy conserved) with inelastic collisions (momentum only conserved). |
| **WA-PHY-MCH-09** | Principle of Moments & Conditions for Rigid Body Equilibrium | SS 1-3, WAEC, JAMB | WAEC Chief Examiner Report (Physics 2 & 3) 2018-2023 | High | **Yes** |
| *Description* | Calculating torque without perpendicular distance from the line of action of the force to the pivot ($M = F \times d_\perp$). Forgetting the weight of non-uniform or uniform meter rules acting through their centre of gravity. |
| **WA-PHY-MCH-10** | Fluid Pressure, Archimedes' Principle & Law of Flotation | SS 1-3, WAEC, JAMB | WAEC Chief Examiner Report 2022; JAMB UTME Review | High | **Yes** |
| *Description* | Confusing gauge pressure ($P = \rho g h$) with absolute pressure ($P_{\text{abs}} = P_0 + \rho g h$). Incorrectly equating upthrust to the weight of the floating object rather than the weight of displaced liquid for partially submerged bodies. |
| **WA-PHY-MCH-11** | Hooke's Law, Elastic Limit & Young's Modulus of Elasticity | SS 1-3, WAEC, JAMB | WAEC Chief Examiner Report 2021; NECO SSCE | High | No |
| *Description* | Forgetting extension $e = l - l_0$ rather than absolute stretched length. Confusing tensile stress ($\sigma = F/A$) and tensile strain ($\epsilon = e/L_0$) with Young's Modulus ($E = \frac{FL_0}{Ae}$), or using diameter instead of radius for cross-sectional area ($\pi r^2$). |
| **WA-PHY-MCH-12** | Simple Harmonic Motion (SHM): Pendulum & Loaded Spring | SS 2-3, WAEC, JAMB | WAEC Chief Examiner Report 2020-2023; JAMB IBASS | High | **Yes** |
| *Description* | Incorrect relationship between period and mass or length ($T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{l}{g}}$, $T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{m}{k}}$). Forgetting that restoring acceleration is maximum at extreme displacement while speed is maximum at equilibrium position. |
